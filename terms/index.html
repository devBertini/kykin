KyKin helps the people who care for one person keep their medicines, stock, appointments, tasks, notes and measurements in one place, shared through their own iCloud. It is provided "as is", for personal use.

Not medical advice

KyKin is a record-keeping notebook, not a medical service or device. It registers what a doctor prescribed and what you enter. Nothing in it is a diagnosis, a treatment or a dosing recommendation, and its stock forecast is arithmetic on the numbers you typed. Always follow your doctor and the medicine's leaflet. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Reminders are not a safety system

Reminders depend on the device, its settings, Focus modes, battery and the system, and may arrive late or not at all. Do not rely on KyKin as the only way to give a medicine on time. Check the records against what was actually given.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you and your circle record, and for who you invite. Members you invite can see the person's records and who marked each dose. Sharing is through Apple's iCloud and depends on its availability and on each member's Apple Account and iCloud storage.

Leaving and ending a share

A member can leave a circle at any time. The person who started the share can stop it, and then every other member loses access to the history; this is said before it is confirmed. Deleting all data removes the records from every member's iPhone and from iCloud, and cannot be undone.

Subscriptions and purchases

KyKin Pro is available as a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription (a 7-day free trial may be offered) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings. Any unused part of a free trial ends when you buy a subscription.

Pro belongs to the circle: when any member buys it, it unlocks Pro for that person's whole circle. If Pro ends, nobody is removed and nothing is deleted; the people and members beyond the free plan's limit become read-only until Pro returns. The free plan covers 1 person, unlimited medicines, stock and agenda, and 1 other caregiver, counting every member.
